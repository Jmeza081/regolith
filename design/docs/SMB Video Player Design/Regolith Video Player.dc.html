REGOLITH
VIDEO PLAYER
The design system for the SMB video player, and the app drawn in it. Black ground with a single red reserved for the one action a screen wants, Michroma over Space Grotesk, hairline surfaces at 14px, and a floating frosted nav pill over four destinations that content runs under. A components library, then thirty-seven screens across twelve sections — every state the app has, including the ones that fail.

12 · RESEARCH BACKLOG
What the 2026 field ships that this does not yet — read against Jellyfin, Plex, Emby, the VLC/MX-class local players and the small paid iOS ones. Continue watching, search and offline downloads came out of this list and are drawn above; these five are not built.
CAST & PICTURE-IN-PICTUREMISSING
The living-room TV is where most of this content wants to end up, and PiP is standard on phone players. Both are player-bar affordances — cheap to add, loud when absent.
Plex · the paid local players
AUDIO & SUBTITLE TRACKSCUT FOR NOW
Switching embedded tracks mid-playback, sideloading an SRT, nudging sync. Drawn once and pulled as out of scope — the largest single thing this player will be measured against when it returns.
VLC · MX Player · every media server
SLEEP TIMERCONSIDER
Speed we have and A–B repeat now ships in the playback sheet. Its last neighbour — a timer that actually pauses playback rather than dimming the screen — is what the focused paid players charge for, and belongs in the same sheet.
MX Player · the paid iOS players
SKIP INTRO · NEXT UPCONSIDER
Intro and credit detection sits behind Plex Pass and is among the most-cited reasons people pay. For a series folder, autoplay of the next file plus a skip chip is a two-element change to the player already drawn.
Plex Pass · Jellyfin plugins
PRIVATE FOLDERCONSIDER
The privacy-first crowd is exactly who picks a self-hosted share over a streaming service — no account, no telemetry, nothing leaving the network. A PIN-hidden folder and a keep-no-history switch answer that positioning directly.
Local players and Jellyfin both lead with it
