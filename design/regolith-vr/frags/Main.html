The spatial model

Four things exist in the space. Seen from above, with you at the centre.

[[headset]]

Screen · 2–12 m · the video, nothing on it

Wall · 1.5 m · library panels

Bar · 1 m · controls

You

Dock · under the Wall

What each one is for

[[grid]]WallHome, Library, Search, Browse, Settings. Curved around you, slightly below eye level.
[[home]]DockThe nav pill. Five tabs. Becomes a toolbar when you select things.
[[size]]ScreenThe picture. Any size, flat or curved, in a void, a theatre or your room.
[[play]]BarTransport, chapters, Relief. Hidden while you watch; a trigger or pinch brings it back.

Three rules

Nothing is drawn over the picture except subtitles.

One red thing per view.

The Wall and the Screen are never both full size: playing puts the Wall away, browsing docks the Screen.
