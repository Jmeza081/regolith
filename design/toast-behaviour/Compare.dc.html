Why it looks orphaned
Nothing is wrong with the snackbar. The pill and the message are on two independent clocks, and the message outlives the pill — so for the last second or so it sits on 112dp of reserved nothing.
0.0s
Move finishes, snackbar appears. Showing it is not a touch, so the pill's idle timer is never reset.
3.0s
RAIL_IDLE_MS elapses — the pill slides out. LocalNavPillInsets keeps returning a constant 112dp, so nothing reflows.
4.0s+
SnackbarDuration.Short (4000ms) ends — later still if the OS extends it for accessibility.
the gap
~1s of a card floating above empty space. Longer on a slow batch, and every time on a device with a longer recommended timeout.
Three ways out
A · Keep them on one clock
The message holds the pill open for as long as it is up, and the inset animates rather than jumping. Stays where Material puts a snackbar.
Cheapest: LocalNavChromeVisible already exists and Shorts already reads it. Keeps Undo in the thumb's reach.
But: the pill lingering is itself a change, and the message still covers the thing it is about.
B · Inline strip at the top
Sits under the title and pushes the list down, the way the “Couldn't reach media” card already does. Can persist until dismissed.
Never collides with the pill or the thumb zone, and a message that must not be missed can simply stay.
But: it reflows the list, and Undo ends up at the far end of the screen from the thumb.
C · Floating toast at the top
Same corner as B but overlaid, so nothing reflows. Auto-dismisses like a snackbar does today.
No collision with the pill, no layout movement, and it reads as a notification — which is what the top of the screen already means on Android.
But: it covers the top of the list, and it is a new surface to own rather than one the app already has.
What I would do
A and B together, split by whether the message matters. Transient confirmations that carry Undo stay at the bottom and hold the pill open (A) — Undo belongs under the thumb, and Material is explicit that a snackbar may be nudged up to clear navigation. Anything the user must not miss — a partial move, a read-only share — goes to the inline strip (B) and stays until dismissed, which Browse already does with its own banner. The rule to keep: if it is acceptable for the message to fade, it is acceptable for it to have been missed.
Where this comes from
Material Components: setAnchorView exists to place a snackbar above bottom navigation · M3: a snackbar may be nudged up and must not cover navigation · Compose SnackbarHost: Short = 4000ms, Long = 10000ms, extended by the platform's recommended accessibility timeout, one at a time · Atlassian: never auto-dismiss anything critical · Carbon: confirmations 2–3s, informational 4–6s.
